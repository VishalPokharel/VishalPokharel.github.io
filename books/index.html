Work Reading Blogs

Hooked: How to Build Habit-Forming Products

Nir Eyal with Ryan Hoover

I read this while working on Liart.io during LOCUS. The useful part for me was not treating attention as magic. It gave me language for feedback loops, triggers, rewards, and why people come back to products.

The Mom Test

Rob Fitzpatrick

This book was useful because it made customer conversations feel less mystical. The main idea I took from it was to ask about real behavior and past actions instead of asking people to validate my idea.

Zero to One

Peter Thiel with Blake Masters

This book pushed me to think about what kind of company is worth building, not just what kind of product can be built. I read it early, when I was still trying to understand startups from first principles.

Persuasion: The Art of Influencing

James Borg

This connected directly to how I thought about the door-to-door sales experience: trust cues, reciprocity, framing, contrast, and why a person can feel pulled toward a decision faster than they realize.

Talking to Humans

Giff Constable

This sits close to The Mom Test for me. It is a reminder that customer discovery is not about asking people to approve an idea, but about learning from real behavior, constraints, and repeated conversations.

Build: An Unorthodox Guide to Making Things Worth Making

Tony Fadell

I like this as a practical product-building book: taste, teams, deadlines, and the stubborn work of making something useful enough that people actually care.

The Lean Startup

Eric Ries

The useful idea here is not just moving fast. It is building in a way that turns effort into learning, and being honest about whether an experiment taught anything real.

Autobiography of a Yogi

Paramahansa Yogananda

A spiritual autobiography about discipline, devotion, and inner life. It is a different kind of reading from the product books, but it belongs here because it shaped how many people think about attention and practice.

The Book of Man

Osho

A set of reflections on masculinity, conditioning, awareness, and the roles people inherit before they understand themselves.

The Book of Woman

Osho

A companion to The Book of Man, focused on feminine energy, freedom, conditioning, love, and awareness.

From Sex to Superconsciousness

Osho

This book explores sexuality, awareness, and the movement from instinct toward consciousness. It is one of the more unusual entries in the reading shelf.

Fear

Osho

A book about fear, insecurity, and learning to live with uncertainty instead of pretending it can be removed completely.
